The Problem

Academic announcements are scattered across different platforms.

📢 Scattered Announcements

Students receive updates from Messenger, LMS, and classroom group chats.

⏰ Missed Deadlines

Important quizzes, exams, and presentations are easily forgotten.

📚 No Central Organizer

Students manually organize schedules, reminders, and requirements.

ARKOBOMBS Features

Everything students need in one organized platform.

📅 Smart Calendar

View quizzes, exams, deadlines, and events in one calendar.

🔔 Notification Center

Receive announcements instantly from teachers.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Portal

Teachers can post announcements and update schedules.

📚 Subject Organizer

Announcements are grouped by subject.

⏳ Countdown Widget

See how many days remain before an exam or presentation.

🚨 Emergency Alerts

Receive suspension, weather, and campus emergency notifications.

How ARKOBOMBS Works

Teacher announces → ARKOBOMBS organizes → Student acts.

1️⃣ Teacher Creates Announcement

Posts quizzes, laboratory exams, presentations, and reminders.

2️⃣ ARKOBOMBS Synchronizes

The announcement automatically appears on the dashboard, calendar, and notifications.

3️⃣ Student Receives Reminder

Students stay updated and prepared from one organized portal.
